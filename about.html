Our Story

A Legacy of Compassion

MyKohi was born from love. Specifically, the love and spirit of Kohi, Dr. Karan Narwal's beloved rescue pup, whose gentle nature and unwavering compassion touched every life she encountered.

Kohi reminded us that kindness has the power to change lives. That by showing up with empathy, patience, and purpose, we can create meaningful connections and lasting impact. Those values became the foundation of MyKohi and continue to shape the way we serve the mental health community.

MyKohi also honors the memory of Buddy, whose warmth, loyalty, and unconditional love continue to inspire the compassion, resilience, and sense of family that define our team.
