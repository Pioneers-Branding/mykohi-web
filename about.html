Our Story

Dr. Karan Narwal

Founder & President

Dr. Karan brings over a decade of experience in the mental health community as a clinical researcher, treatment specialist, and business executive. A graduate of Aureus University School of Medicine and the New York Institute of Technology, Dr. Karan has dedicated his career to advancing mental health care through innovation and leadership.

Under his guidance as part owner of Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) treatment facilities, he built one of Tampa Bay's leading mental health centers, delivering more than 15,000 transformative treatments. As Medical Director for Beyond Marketing, he supported providers in mental health, specializing in TMS and Ketamine, by implementing best practices for patient enrollment and retention. Dr. Karan actively contributes to the field by publishing studies on TMS treatment and serving as a Clinical TMS Society member. Through presentations at conferences, seminars, and podcasts nationwide, he shares insights on emerging trends in treatments like TMS and Ketamine.

With expertise spanning clinical care, business operations, and practice growth, Dr. Karan helps providers elevate patient care and grow sustainable, quality-driven practices. His mission is to empower mental health professionals with the tools, knowledge, and strategies to thrive in a rapidly evolving industry.

Brian Goldbeck

Co-Founder, CEO

Brian Goldbeck's journey reflects a lifelong passion for leadership, growth, and creating meaningful impact. Born and raised in New Jersey, Brian cultivated his drive early on, forming a lasting bond with Dr. Karan Narwal during their time at Christian Brothers Academy. After earning a Bachelor's degree in Business Management from Monmouth University, Brian embarked on a new chapter in Florida with his wife in 2011, where they now reside in Manatee County.

With decades of expertise in sales, business development, and client success, Brian built a career defined by leadership and excellence. Recognized with President's Club honors and MVP awards at multiple organizations, he led sales teams to consistently exceed expectations, generating millions in revenue. Beyond the accolades, Brian's career embodies his drive to contribute to something greater — work that makes a difference. That vision came to life when Brian joined forces with Dr. Karan to launch MyKohi Consulting. With a shared mission to empower mental health providers and transform patient care, they created a firm dedicated to TMS, Ketamine, and Mental Health clinics. Having witnessed the challenges and triumphs of mental health within his own family, Brian approaches this work with deep empathy and personal conviction.

Brian's leadership blends strategic expertise with heart, delivering measurable growth for clinics while helping providers fulfill their mission. At MyKohi, his focus remains steadfast: driving success for mental health professionals and making a lasting impact on the lives of patients and their communities.

Arush Thaper

Chief Marketing Officer

Arush Thaper helps healthcare clinics, hospitals, and B2B SaaS brands grow through digital marketing that converts and AI systems that scale. With 9+ years running Branding Pioneers, he has worked with some of India's top hospital groups (Apollo, Max Healthcare) and clients across the USA and Middle East — specializing in SEO, performance ads, social media, and web development, all built around one goal: measurable growth.

He is now building Rain Mindz, an AI automation company with a full portfolio of healthcare and enterprise SaaS products — including AI voice agents (Altelz), AI chatbots (AITextBotz), AI reputation management (AIRatingz), an AI-powered CRM (AICRMZ), an LLM ranking and audit tool (AILLMZ), AI video generation (AIFilmz), an AI health provider platform (AIHealz), and MedAppz — a suite of AI tools for clinics and hospitals (EHR, HIS, queue management).

Arush's belief: the future of healthcare growth lives at the intersection of trust-driven marketing and intelligent automation. Whether it's helping a clinic rank on Google, automating patient communication, or building AI-powered hospital software, his focus is on solutions that move the needle. Based in Gurugram, working globally.
