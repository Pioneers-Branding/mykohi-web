Common Questions

Common Questions

What is Clinical Advisory & Provider Education?

Clinical Advisory & Provider Education is a collaborative service designed to help mental health practices strengthen treatment awareness, provider confidence, and patient engagement. Through education, strategic guidance, and practical support, MyKohi helps teams identify opportunities to improve the patient journey and expand access to innovative care.

What are Done-For-You (DFY) Consultations?

DFY Consultations are educational treatment conversations conducted through a secure, HIPAA-conscious platform to help patients better understand available treatment options. Our process supports patient education, addresses common questions, and helps practices create a smoother path from inquiry to informed decision-making.

Can this support TMS and Spravato practices?

Absolutely. Our services are designed to support practices offering TMS, Spravato, ketamine, and other innovative mental health treatments by strengthening provider awareness, patient education, referral pathways, and practice growth strategies.

Do you work with existing provider teams?

Yes. We work alongside existing practice owners, providers, and support staff to complement the systems and workflows already in place. Our goal is to strengthen collaboration, improve communication, and help teams operate more effectively together.

Can educational workshops be virtual or onsite?

Yes. Educational sessions can be delivered virtually or onsite depending on your practice's needs. Workshops are customized to your goals and may focus on treatment awareness, patient engagement, referral development, team communication, and operational best practices.

How do we know if this service is right for our practice?

Every practice has unique goals and challenges. We begin with a conversation to understand your needs and determine where MyKohi can provide the greatest value. Whether you're looking to strengthen provider education, improve patient engagement, or support practice growth, we'll help identify the right approach.

Can MyKohi customize education and advisory services for our practice?

Yes. No two practices are alike. Our services are tailored to your team's experience level, treatment offerings, operational goals, and long-term vision to provide practical solutions that fit your unique needs.

What makes MyKohi different?

MyKohi combines real-world experience in mental healthcare, practice growth, marketing, and operations to provide a collaborative approach that goes beyond traditional consulting. We focus on helping practices strengthen their teams, improve patient experiences, and create sustainable opportunities for long-term success.
