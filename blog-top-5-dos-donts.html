In the evolving world of mental health treatment, TMS and Ketamine clinics are at the forefront of innovative care. But while the demand for these services continues to grow, many clinics struggle with operational, marketing, and patient management challenges. At MyKohi, we specialize in helping clinics navigate these complexities to build a thriving practice. If you’re a provider offering TMS, Ketamine therapy, medication management, and psychotherapy, here are the top five things your clinic should and shouldn’t do to ensure access to quality care.

The Do’s: Strategies for Growth & Success For Your Clinic

1. Do Prioritize Patient Education & Expectation Management

TMS and Ketamine therapy are still relatively new to many patients. Educating them on the science, expected outcomes, and treatment timelines is crucial. Make sure your website, intake process, and staff are equipped with clear, digestible information. Setting realistic expectations prevents patient frustration and boosts retention.

2. Do Streamline Your Patient Intake & Communication Process

Clinics often lose potential patients due to poor follow-up and inefficient intake procedures. Implement a strong CRM system, automate follow-ups, and ensure staff are well-trained in converting inquiries into scheduled consultations. At MyKohi Consulting, we help clinics optimize patient communication for higher conversion rates and increased engagement.

3. Do Invest in Targeted Marketing, Online Presence, and Referrals

Your potential patients are searching for solutions online. If your website is outdated or your digital marketing strategy is weak, you’re missing out on valuable leads. Focus on SEO, Google Ads, and social media campaigns tailored to your local audience. We specialize in building high-converting marketing strategies specifically for mental health clinics. Targeted marketing also includes building referral networks. Marketing is outreach, so having a strategy that is focused on referral development and nurture is equally important.

4. Do Offer a Comprehensive Treatment Approach

Patients seeking TMS or Ketamine therapy often require ongoing medication management and psychotherapy. A well-rounded treatment plan that integrates these services increases patient outcomes and revenue. Ensure your team collaborates effectively, and highlight the benefits of a holistic approach in your marketing.

5. Do Track Key Performance Metrics & Adjust Accordingly

Successful clinics don’t rely on guesswork. Monitor appointment conversion rates, patient retention, treatment success rates, and ROI on marketing efforts. Regular data analysis allows for informed decision-making and sustainable growth. MyKohi helps clinics implement data-driven strategies that maximize efficiency and profitability.

---

The Don’ts: Mistakes That Could Hurt Your Clinic

1. Don’t Overpromise Results or Make Unrealistic Claims

TMS and Ketamine therapy can be life-changing, but they’re not magic cures. Avoid marketing messages that guarantee results or claim instant transformation. Not only is this unethical, but it can also lead to disappointed patients and potential legal issues. Stick to evidence-based communication.

2. Don’t Ignore Compliance & Documentation Best Practices

Regulatory compliance in mental health treatment is strict. Ensure proper documentation for informed consent, treatment plans, and follow-ups. Many clinics struggle with compliance-related issues, which can lead to audits or legal complications. We help clinics establish foolproof documentation protocols to avoid these risks.

3. Don’t Neglect Staff Training & Development

Your team is your clinic’s backbone. If they aren’t properly trained in patient care, treatment protocols, and communication strategies, your reputation will suffer. Invest in continuous education, especially for front-desk staff and patient coordinators who manage first impressions.

4. Don’t Ignore the Power of Patient Testimonials & Referrals

Positive patient experiences should translate into reviews and referrals. Don’t miss out on the opportunity to encourage satisfied patients to leave Google reviews or share their stories (with consent). A strong referral program can drive new business without additional marketing costs.

5. Don’t Assume a One-Size-Fits-All Approach Works

Each clinic’s market, patient demographic, and competitive landscape are unique. Copy-pasting another clinic’s strategy won’t necessarily yield the same results. This is why we tailor our consulting services to each client’s specific needs, ensuring a custom roadmap to success.

Running a successful TMS and Ketamine clinic requires more than just great clinical care. It demands a strategic approach to operations, marketing, and patient experience. At MyKohi, we partner with clinics to implement best practices, streamline processes, and drive sustainable growth. Whether you need help with patient acquisition, operational efficiency, or compliance, our expert team is here to guide you.
