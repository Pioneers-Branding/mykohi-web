Fix first

Critical

Critical

How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. Findings come from a full crawl of all 631 sitemap URLs on 10 Nov 2026.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile homepage: Performance 71, Accessibility 84, Best Practices 78, SEO 92. It tests basics only. All sitemap URLs load, HTTPS redirects work, and canonical tags are correct. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed

Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks, Google Business Profile, reviews on third-party sites and real-user Core Web Vitals. These need account or tool access and are not part of the score.
