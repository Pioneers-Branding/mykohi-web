Fix first

  1. Retire or update the 2019–2020 posts, starting with the COVID articles, and publish regularly again.
  2. Take down or rewrite the CBD Oil page, removing the "safe" and "proven benefits" claims. Fix the aesthetics page that copies its title.
  3. Give each service and each physician its own page: weight loss, urgent care, pediatrics, functional medicine and the five doctors.
  4. Add MedicalClinic and Physician schema with the Newberry address, and target Newberry and Alachua in titles and copy.
  5. Install Google Analytics and track enrollments, and confirm that Google's crawler is not blocked by the site's bot protection.
  6. Shorten titles, add H1s to Contact, FAQ and the blog posts, and stop the Vimeo player loading on the homepage until clicked.

Content & medical trust

Critical

High

The CBD Oil page calls CBD "natural and safe" with "proven benefits" for anxiety, sleep and pain, and promotes one supplier's product. These are FDA and FTC risk claims for a physician practice. /cbd-oil, which has no internal links.

High

The Dysport and Restylane aesthetics page has no internal links and reuses the CBD page's title and description ("CBD OIL | Gainesville...").

High

Eight menu services have no page of their own: pediatrics, adult and GYN, urgent care, in-house dispensary, lab and radiology, functional medicine, counseling, and weight loss. They are sections on just 2 pages.

Medium

The TMS page lists "Anxiety" as an FDA-approved TMS indication. FDA clearance covers anxiety symptoms within depression, not anxiety disorders, and its outcome figures ("up to 30% remission, up to 80% improvement") have no source.

How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. Findings come from a full crawl of all 21 sitemap URLs plus the root on 15 Nov 2026.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile scores were Performance 34, Accessibility 87, Best Practices 78 and SEO 79 on the homepage, and 43, 86, 78 and 79 on Pricing. Every page loads, HTTPS and the bare domain redirect in one hop, and canonicals are correct. Security headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options, nosniff) are set, the phone and address are consistent, and membership prices are published. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed

Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks, Google Business Profile, third-party reviews and real-user Core Web Vitals were not assessed. They need account or tool access and are not part of the score. Whether Googlebot itself is blocked was not tested. FDA, FTC and HIPAA points are flags for review, not legal advice.
