Fix first

  1. Make the Florida claim match the providers. Show which clinicians are licensed in Florida, or remove "Florida" from titles and pages until one is.
  2. Link the 30 condition pages from a real Services page, the menu and related blog posts.
  3. Clean the sitemap. Drop 602 attachment redirects, theme templates, the form post and 2 dead URLs.
  4. Name a clinician author or reviewer on all 110 blog posts, and show 988 on every page.
  5. Close the WordPress leaks: hide the public user list, add security headers, and turn off XML-RPC.
  6. Cut homepage load time: load reCAPTCHA once, remove the duplicate video, and fix the slow server response.

How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. Findings come from a full crawl of all page, post and template sitemaps (208 URLs) plus a sample of attachment URLs on 13 Nov 2026.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile scores were Performance 30, Accessibility 98, Best Practices 74 and SEO 100 on the homepage, and 63, 98, 74 and 93 on a condition page. HTTPS and www redirect in one hop. Every page has a title, a description, a canonical and Open Graph tags. Condition pages run 1,150 to 1,550 words with research citations. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed

Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks, Google Business Profile, third-party reviews and real-user Core Web Vitals were not assessed. They need account or tool access and are not part of the score. Licensing and HIPAA points are flags for review, not legal advice.
