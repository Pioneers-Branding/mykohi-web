How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. Findings come from a full crawl of all 112 sitemap URLs (41 pages and 71 posts) on 20 Nov 2026, plus checks on 199 outbound links.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile scores were Performance 39, Accessibility 90, Best Practices 78 and SEO 100 on the homepage, and 53, 86, 78 and 100 on TMS. Every URL loads, canonicals are correct, and the phone and address are consistent. Condition pages are 2,000+ words with FAQ schema, every image has alt text, and 988 appears on 15 pages. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed

Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks, Google Business Profile, third-party reviews and real-user Core Web Vitals were not assessed. They need account or tool access and are not part of the score. The intake form tool itself was not identified. HIPAA points are flags for review, not legal advice.
