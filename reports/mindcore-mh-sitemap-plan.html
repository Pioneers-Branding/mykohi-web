What the new sitemap has to fix

  • The site markets "New York & Florida" care, but both listed psychiatrists are described as New York only. Re-checked 23 Nov: unchanged.
  • No page links to the 30 condition pages; Google finds them only through the sitemap. Re-checked 23 Nov: unchanged.
  • There is no Contact page, no Services page and no individual doctor pages.
  • 110 blog posts have no named clinician author and all sit in "Uncategorized".
  • 602 attachment redirects, theme templates and dead URLs fill the sitemap.
  • The homepage takes 20.8 s to show its main content on mobile.
What this plan does. It keeps every page that already earns its place, rebuilds the pages that need it, and adds the pages patients and Google expect from a telepsychiatry practice: a real Services hub, a Conditions hub that finally links the 30 condition pages, doctor profiles, location pages, medication guides and the trust pages a health site needs. Every new page is listed with its section-by-section outline and exactly what we need from the practice to build it.

Today the H1 is "Live without anxiety or exhaustion" and Services goes to /#services.

  • Brand colours, fonts and any taglines to keep

How to use this plan

Florida pages are conditional on a Florida-licensed clinician; until then all Florida claims should be removed. Child and adolescent pages are conditional on hiring a clinician who sees under-18s. Existing URLs are kept wherever possible to protect rankings; any merged URL gets a 301 redirect.
