What the new sitemap has to fix

  • 158 of 600 pages have no internal links, including 22 of 24 neighbourhood pages and 11 clinician profiles.
  • 24 Brooklyn neighbourhood pages and about 30 upstate "service in city" pages are templated near-copies for a telehealth-only practice (doorway risk).
  • The same topic often exists 2–4 times (grief, bereavement, divorce, couples, cyclothymia), splitting rankings.
  • Clinician credentials conflict (an "MD" URL for an LMSW; duplicate profiles), and blog posts name no clinician.
  • Every footer still reads "Copyright © 2023 LocalMD", and 347 tag archives fill the sitemap.
  • The Meta Pixel runs on condition pages, and the homepage takes 22.9 s to show its main content on mobile.
Fixed since the audit (re-checked 23 Nov): the Patient Portal link now works, every page shows one phone number (718-307-1577), and the FAQ placeholder text is gone.
What this plan does. This site already has about 600 URLs, so more pages alone won't help. The plan merges duplicate and templated pages into stronger ones, then adds the pages that are genuinely missing: an About page, booking, telehealth and first-visit guides, fees, reviews, crisis resources, hubs for therapy approaches, counseling topics and locations, Queens pages near the Maspeth address, a Spanish section and medication guides. Every page lists its section-by-section outline and exactly what we need from the practice.
