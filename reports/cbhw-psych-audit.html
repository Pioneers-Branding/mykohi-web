Fix first

  1. Let Google's ad crawler back in. Squarespace's AI-crawler blocking also blocks AdsBot-Google, which can get ads disapproved.
  2. Use one phone number everywhere: header, footer, schema, Contact page and Google Business Profile.
  3. Fill the Reviews page with real reviews or remove it, and fix the Records Release form link that returns 404.
  4. Show the 988 crisis line on every page, and give the psychiatrist's full name and MD credentials.
  5. Review the Meta Pixel and Squarespace forms with HIPAA counsel, since they run on pages that collect insurance and treatment interest.
  6. Speed up mobile pages and build reach: cut blocking scripts, load YouTube on click, merge the two TMS pages, and add condition and Columbia pages.

30/100

How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. Findings come from a full crawl of all 21 sitemap URLs plus the root on 11 Nov 2026.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile scores were Performance 31, Accessibility 90, Best Practices 74 and SEO 100 on the homepage, and 37, 86, 74 and 100 on TMS. All URLs load, HTTPS and www redirect in one hop, and every page has a canonical, Open Graph tags and a viewport tag. The four main service pages are 1,400 to 1,700 words with the city in the title. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed

Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks, Google Business Profile, third-party reviews and real-user Core Web Vitals were not assessed. They need account or tool access and are not part of the score. HIPAA points are flags for review, not legal advice.
