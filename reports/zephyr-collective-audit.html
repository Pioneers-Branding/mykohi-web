Fix first

  1. Relabel or remove the first testimonial and confirm every quote comes from an actual participant.
  2. Update the launch wording. Replace "Opening August 2026" and "Calendar coming soon" across the footer, schema and Programs page.
  3. Correct the licensing statement to name the Department of Revenue's Natural Medicine Division, and show the healing center license number.
  4. Build pages people search for: psilocybin therapy for depression, PTSD and end-of-life distress, Denver-area pages, and a blog.
  5. Fix the plumbing: force HTTPS on http://www, link to the trailing-slash URLs, and install analytics.
  6. Show the full address and phone on every page, and add the 988 crisis line.

Content & medical trust

25/100
Critical

The first homepage testimonial is labeled "Individual journey participant", but it describes working with Emily and Annmarie "in an acute outpatient setting" and their care "for their patients". That reads as a colleague's reference, and FTC endorsement rules require honest labels.

High

The launch wording is out of date. The homepage says "Now open as of August 2026", but the footer on all 10 pages and the schema say "Opening August 2026", and Programs still says "Calendar coming soon".

High

The FAQ says the practice holds "a full licence issued by... DORA, which oversees all natural medicine health services". Colorado healing center licenses come from the Department of Revenue's Natural Medicine Division; DORA licenses facilitators. No license number is shown anywhere.

High

There is no crisis number on a site discussing depression and PTSD. The FAQ only says "contact a mental health crisis line". 988 appears on 0 of 10 pages.

High

There is too little content to rank: no pages for depression, PTSD, anxiety, end-of-life distress or addiction, and no blog. 10 pages in total, 3 of them legal or contact pages.

Medium

British spellings are used for a Colorado audience: "centre" (15 times), "licence", "practising", "specialises", "enquiry" and "legalised".

Technical & crawl health

45/100
High

High

Every menu and footer link points to a URL without the trailing slash, which then redirects with a temporary 307. The real page URLs receive no direct internal links. 9 links on all 10 pages.

High

Medium

robots.txt contradicts itself. Cloudflare's managed block disallows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended, and the site's own rules lower down allow them. Lighthouse marks the file invalid.

Medium

How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. Findings come from a full crawl of all 10 sitemap URLs on 15 Nov 2026.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile scores were Performance 70, Accessibility 95, Best Practices 100 and SEO 93 on the homepage, and 86, 95, 100 and 93 on Programs. The server responds in about 120 ms, and pages load no third-party scripts. Every page has a unique title, a canonical, Open Graph tags and MedicalOrganization schema, and pricing is published. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed

Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks, Google Business Profile, third-party reviews and real-user Core Web Vitals were not assessed. They need account or tool access and are not part of the score. Licensing, FTC and HIPAA points are flags for review, not legal advice.
