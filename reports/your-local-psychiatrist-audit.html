How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. The crawl on 13 Nov 2026 covered every page, provider, category and author URL and 284 of 291 posts. Tag archives were counted but not crawled.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile scores were Performance 39, Accessibility 89, Best Practices 78 and SEO 100 on the homepage, and 46, 88, 78 and 93 on Services. HTTPS and www redirect in one hop. Every page has a title, a canonical, Open Graph tags and breadcrumbs. Condition pages are long and cite NIH sources. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed

Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks, Google Business Profile, third-party reviews and real-user Core Web Vitals were not assessed. They need account or tool access and are not part of the score. HIPAA points are flags for review, not legal advice.
