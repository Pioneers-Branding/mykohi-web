Fix first

  1. Name the clinician. Put Tiana King, DNP, PMHNP-BC, with photo and state licenses, on About and the homepage.
  2. Pick one URL per page. Redirect .html to the clean URL, add canonicals, and rebuild the sitemap with all real pages.
  3. Publish the blog and launch or hide the "Coming Soon" Lifestyle program. Rewrite the four state pages so each is genuinely state-specific.
  4. Make the privacy policy match the site, or load the booking widget only when someone clicks "Request Appointment".

Content & medical trust

Critical

The About page lists degrees, a DNP and PMHNP credentials, but never says whose they are. It refers only to "the founder" and "her journey". The clinician's name, Tiana King, appears on just 2 of 27 pages: the forensic evaluation page and the medical disclaimer.

High

The blog has no articles: every post listed, including the featured one, is marked "Coming Soon". The Lifestyle Medicine Program in the main menu is also "Coming Soon".

High

The state pages are near-copies of the homepage. Google may treat them as doorway pages. Florida, Arizona, Massachusetts and DC are 95–98% word-for-word identical to the homepage, with the same H1.

High

The "Why Plant-Based?" page says animal products are "recognized as a key driver of depression, anxiety, cognitive decline, and even psychosis". That claim is overstated for a psychiatry practice. 4,163 words.

How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. Findings come from a crawl on 17 Nov 2026 of all 14 sitemap URLs, the 11 pages the sitemap leaves out, and 2 duplicate URLs.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile scores were Performance 93, Accessibility 94, Best Practices 74 and SEO 92 on the homepage, and 96, 93, 74 and 100 on Medication Management. HTTPS is enforced, the phone number is consistent, insurance is listed by state, and the 988 crisis line is in every footer. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed

Keyword rankings, organic traffic, backlinks, Google Business Profile, third-party reviews and real-user Core Web Vitals were not assessed. They need account or tool access and are not part of the score. Licensing and privacy points are flags for review, not legal advice.
