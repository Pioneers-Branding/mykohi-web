Fix first

  1. Remove the hidden gambling links today. Run a malware scan, update plugins and change all WordPress passwords.
  2. Stop filtering reviews by star rating. Offer every patient the same public review link.
  3. Fix the TMS Therapy link and publish a real TMS page on this site.
  4. Cut homepage load time. Compress the main image, and reduce the 50 stylesheets and 29 blocking scripts.
  5. Correct the basics: one spelling of the doctor's name, homepage placeholders, 13 missing descriptions, and clinic and physician schema.
  6. Build local reach with Plano service pages, nearby-city pages and clinician-reviewed articles.

How it was scored

Each area was graded strictly on the evidence, then weighted. Medical trust and content carry the most weight because this is health information. Findings come from a full crawl of all 23 sitemap URLs on 10 Nov 2026.

Lab results and basics that passed

Lighthouse mobile homepage: Performance 56, Accessibility 95, Best Practices 74, SEO 79. All sitemap URLs load, HTTPS redirects work, canonicals are correct, and a Notice of Privacy Practices is published. Speed was tested in one simulated run.

Not assessed
