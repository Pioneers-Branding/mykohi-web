FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of practices do you work with?

We primarily support mental health, psychiatry, TMS, ketamine, behavioral health, and addiction treatment practices. Whether you are launching a new clinic, expanding into multiple locations, or looking to improve patient acquisition and operational performance, our team provides tailored marketing, consulting, and growth solutions designed specifically for healthcare practices.

How long does it take to see results?

Every practice is different. Some operational and conversion improvements can produce measurable gains within weeks, while SEO and organic growth strategies typically take several months to build momentum. Our goal is to help practices achieve both short-term wins and sustainable long-term growth through a balanced approach to marketing, patient acquisition, and business development.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. We believe the strongest results come from long-term partnerships, not one-time projects. Depending on your goals, we offer ongoing marketing support, strategic consulting, staff training, practice development guidance, and growth advisory services to help your practice continue evolving as market conditions change.

What is included in the complimentary practice assessment?

Our complimentary practice assessment is a strategic review of your current marketing, patient acquisition process, online presence, operations, and growth opportunities. During the assessment, we identify strengths, uncover potential gaps, and provide actionable recommendations designed to help your practice attract more patients, improve efficiency, and support long-term growth.

Why should I choose MyKohi instead of a traditional marketing agency?

Most marketing agencies focus exclusively on advertising and lead generation. MyKohi combines marketing expertise with real-world practice ownership, operational consulting, staff training, patient acquisition strategy, and growth advisory services. Our goal is not simply to generate leads, it is to help practices build stronger, healthier, and more sustainable businesses.

MyKohi partners with mental health practices to strengthen brands, attract the right patients, and build sustainable growth through integrated marketing and consulting.
